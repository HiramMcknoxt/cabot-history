Photographs of Cabot

Historic photographs of Cabot and its people, gathered from the Encyclopedia of Arkansas. Every photograph is shown with its caption and credit line exactly as the Encyclopedia publishes them, and each caption links back to the photograph's own Encyclopedia page. The photographs remain the property of their credited holders; they are gathered here for local-history reference by a non-commercial community project.

Contribute to the Repository

This archive grows one piece at a time, and some of the best pieces are sitting in Cabot attics, albums, and desk drawers. If you have a piece of Cabot history, you can share it here.

What we're looking for

  • Photographs — streets, schools, teams, churches, businesses, events, buildings, or just everyday Cabot life, any era.
  • Documents — programs, letters, maps, menus, tickets, flyers, reports, yearbook pages, anything printed or written about Cabot.
  • Newspaper clippings — especially from the old Cabot Star-Herald, whose run survives only on library microfilm.
  • Written memories — a paragraph about a place, a person, or an event is a contribution too. Type it into the form, or upload it as a document.
  • Oral histories — a recorded conversation with someone who remembers the Cabot that is gone. A phone recording is fine. New to interviewing? Read Collecting an Oral History, our short guide to interviewing the older people in your life — then upload the recording the same way.

How it works

  1. Upload through the form below — the file, your name, and whatever you can tell us about it.
  2. Review. Nothing is posted automatically. A person reads every submission first, and we email you with any questions.
  3. Indexing and posting. Approved items are indexed — the text is extracted so they become searchable alongside the council records — and posted with credit to you, exactly as you specify on the form (your name, or anonymous). Oral-history recordings are transcribed first, and the transcript is what gets indexed.

Please only share material you have the right to share. Scans or clear photos of documents are perfect — keep your originals. Depending on copyright, some contributed items (newspaper clippings in particular) may be indexed and described rather than reproduced in full.

Ask a question

Want to know something this site doesn't answer yet — who held a seat in a given year, when the council took a particular vote, what a neighborhood fight was about?

Questions are researched from the same corpus this site is built on. Each answer is drafted with its sources, reviewed, and only then published here with the records cited. Nothing is published from memory or guesswork: if the records don't answer it, the answer says so and names the gap.

Ask via GitHub Issues

A public reference built from Cabot's public records and local news indexes. Council and other government documents are reproduced in full because they are public records. Newspaper and TV coverage appears as links and short summaries only. Resident-discussion material is paraphrased themes, never verbatim posts.
